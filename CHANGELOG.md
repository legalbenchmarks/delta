# Changelog

All notable changes to the dataset are recorded here. The dataset follows
semantic versioning: major = tasks added or removed, minor = criteria
added, removed, or substantively revised, patch = fixes that do not change
what any criterion tests. Always cite the version you measured on.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-08-30

- Added descriptive dimensions for legal correctness, legal judgment, style,
  and usability. The written criteria remain the sole grading standard.
- Separated substantive legal propositions from form requirements in two mixed
  criteria and reclassified two substantive criteria that had been labelled as
  form.
- Reworded form criteria to assess observable presentation of the answer's own
  analysis without prescribing a substantive conclusion.
- Refined three contract-law criteria to state observable legal relationships.
- Reframed task prompts as bounded practitioner assignments without changing
  their legal questions or assessment criteria.
- Added task-level horizon and legal-research cut-off metadata and published the
  fixed system prompt used by the standard harness.
- 15 tasks and 273 criteria: 192 substance, 51 citation, 30 form.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-21

Initial public release of Dutch Legal AI Benchmark (DELTA).

- 15 Dutch legal research tasks across 9 practice areas.
- 271 binary grading criteria: 188 substance, 51 citation, 32 form.
- Dutch normative text with English reference translations throughout.
- The tasks were selected from a collection of more than 200 legal-research
  assignments reflecting questions encountered in Dutch legal practice.
- The public questions and assessment criteria were reviewed by practising
  Dutch lawyers.
- Legal Benchmarks curates and maintains the public benchmark, with Zeno.Law
  as Founding Research Partner.
